# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Qualified scientific reviewers evaluating annotation-auditing research. The owner
specifically wants easier access for an experienced Harvard Medical School
professor. Their identity, device and individual review procedure are unknown;
institutional endorsement and a particular professor's requirements are not assumed.

## Product Purpose

Make the existing AANCA v1 research and its limits understandable and checkable.
The research ranks potentially inconsistent nucleus class annotations recommended
for expert review. It never automatically modifies source annotations.

## Operating Context

The existing public article is hosted at https://aancastudy.org; source, evidence,
protocols and execution history are retained in this repository. Local verification
must work on Windows and Ubuntu. The owner delegated research and implementation
of a professional review path; a browser-first guide and optional local checks
are engineering choices informed by published HMS reproducibility guidance.

## Capabilities and Constraints

`SPEC.md`, frozen protocols and checksum-bound scientific authorities govern
claims. Preserve group-safe out-of-fold predictions, untouched reference groups,
label-field separation, adverse results and the action `retain_uncorrected`.
V1 remains the frozen reference; reviewer access does not execute a new study.
The owner authorised limited non-clinical scientific-evaluation permission, defined
in `LICENSE`. Third-party permissions remain separate.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the existing AANCA identity and the authority of `AANCA_BRAND_SYSTEM.md`.
Use clear English for international reviewers. Harvard branding, endorsement,
fabricated testimonials and third-party validation badges are absent.

## Evidence on Hand

The sealed article, `PROFESSOR_BRIEF.md`, `PUBLIC_EVIDENCE.md`, numeric evidence and
scoped verifiers are available. `PRE_REGISTRATION.md`, `STATUS.md`, `DECISIONS.md`
and `REPRODUCIBILITY.md` explain chronology and limits. No prospective clinical
workflow study or new blinded expert cohort is supplied by this access work.

## Product Principles

- Make important claims traceable to their retained sources.
- Keep negative results and uncertainty visible.
- Describe exactly what each verification command checks.
- Keep the reading path usable without a research environment or account.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The owner requested easier accessibility and verification. Support small screens,
keyboard access, print, reduced motion and a guide that requires no JavaScript.
No particular assistive technology or formal accessibility certification is claimed.
