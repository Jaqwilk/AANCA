# Contributions and AI-assistance disclosure

Natan Smogór defined the research objective, directed the selection and execution of
the experiments, reviewed the retained outputs, and is responsible for the final
scientific claims and presentation decisions in AANCA.

AI-assisted coding tools were used for implementation, debugging, experiment
orchestration, test development, documentation, visual presentation and editorial
work. Their output was not treated as scientific evidence. Retained metrics come
from the saved machine-readable artifacts and governed verification procedures
described in this repository. No AI tool is an author, qualified pathology reviewer,
third-party validator or source of expert labels.

The author reviewed and accepted the retained code, results, limitations and public
claim boundary. Dataset creators, cited researchers and maintainers of third-party
software retain credit for their respective work; citations and software dependencies
are recorded separately.

This disclosure describes the current public repository. It should be updated if
additional human collaborators contribute to study design, pathology review,
statistical analysis or manuscript preparation.
